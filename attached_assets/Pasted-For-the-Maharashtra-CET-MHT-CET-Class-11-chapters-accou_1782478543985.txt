For the Maharashtra CET (MHT-CET), Class 11 chapters account for exactly 20% of the total exam weightage (approx. 10 questions per subject). The remaining 80% comes from Class 12. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]  
The essential Class 11 PCM chapter checklist includes: 
📐 Mathematics 

• Trigonometry II 
• Straight Lines 
• Circle 
• Conic Sections (Includes Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola) 
• Probability 
• Complex Numbers 
• Permutations and Combinations 
• Functions 
• Limits & Continuity [5, 6, 7, 8, 9]  

⚡ Physics 

• Motion in a Plane 
• Laws of Motion (Includes Friction) 
• Gravitation 
• Thermal Properties of Matter 
• Sound 
• Optics 
• Electrostatics 
• Semiconductors 
• Vectors & Error Analysis [7, 10, 11, 12, 13]  

🧪 Chemistry 

• Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry (Mole concept, stoichiometry) 
• Structure of Atom 
• Chemical Bonding 
• Redox Reactions 
• States of Matter (Gaseous and Liquids) 
• Elements of Group 1 & 2 (s-Block) 
• Adsorption & Colloids (Surface Chemistry) 
• Hydrocarbons 
• Basic Principles of Organic Chemistry 

You can verify the detailed subject-wise marks distribution and syllabus deletions directly through the MHT CET Syllabus PDF on the official State CET Cell website. [3, 14, 15]
